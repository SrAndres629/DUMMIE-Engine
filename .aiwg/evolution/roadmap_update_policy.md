# Roadmap Update Policy

1. Roadmap changes must be recorded as `roadmap_delta.json` in future phases.
2. No agent may rewrite the roadmap from chat memory.
3. A phase may be refined when its dependencies are complete.
4. Long-term objectives are stable unless explicitly updated.
5. Short-term objectives may be updated per phase report.
6. Fases lejanas remain compact until they are near execution.
7. Current position must always point to exactly one current phase and one next phase.
8. Deferred capabilities must be registered, not silently discarded.
9. Strategic objections must be recorded with evidence.
